## 🎯 Project Overview — Deliverable 1

**ESMIRIINGHOY** — Maramag Home Food Pre-Order Manager

A simple CRUD web app built for small home-based food sellers in Maramag, Bukidnon. It replaces handwritten notebooks and scattered chat messages with one clean system to manage menu items, customer orders, client profiles, and daily sales.

---

## 📖 Our Journey — Deliverable 1

### 💡 The Problem
Small home-based food sellers currently track orders, stock, and customer details using **handwritten notes or scattered chat messages**. This causes:
- ❌ Lost or forgotten orders
- ❌ Wrong stock counts
- ❌ Missed pickup times
- ❌ No clear sales record

### ✅ Our Solution
A web app that lets food sellers:
- ✅ **Manage Menu** — add, edit, remove food items with prices
- ✅ **Take Orders** — record customer orders with quantity and status
- ✅ **Track Customers** — save contact info for repeat buyers
- ✅ **View Sales** — see daily and total sales
